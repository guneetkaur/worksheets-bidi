## Contingency sheets (generated only when triggered)

- **Gate-fail refresh sheet** — fresh numbers on the failed stage, same difficulty. Trigger: a Friday gate fails.
- **Flash-drill strip** — 10 single sums, 5-second-each format. Trigger: gate failed on time only.
- **Weak-fact drill** — built from `_mistakes.md`. Trigger: the same fact/pattern logged wrong twice.

Budget ~4–8 of these across both tracks; keystone stages are the likely spots.
