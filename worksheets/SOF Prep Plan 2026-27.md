## Paper format (all three, Class 3)

35 subject questions × 1 mark + 5 Achiever's × 3 marks = 50 marks, 60 min, all MCQ.
Every paper opens with a **Logical Reasoning** section — shared skill across all three exams, so it gets its own track.

## Syllabus coverage map

**IEO** — nouns, pronouns, adjectives, verbs & tenses, articles, prepositions, vocabulary (synonym/antonym, odd one out), reading comprehension, spoken/written expression.
Already covered: pronouns, grammar basics. Gap: everything else.

**IMO** — numbers to 9999, addition/subtraction, multiplication/division, fractions, measurement (length/weight/capacity), time, money, geometry basics, data handling.
Already covered: large numbers, addition (mental math track), multiplication started 4 Jul. Gap: division, fractions, measurement, time, money, geometry, data handling.

**NSO** — plants, animals & birds, food, human body, housing & clothing, transport & communication, earth & universe, matter & materials, water & air, light/sound/force.
Already covered: SOF Unit 1 (plants, animals, human body, environment basics). Gap: the rest.

**Logical Reasoning (all three)** — series, analogies, odd one out, ranking, mirror images, embedded figures, coding-decoding, directions.
Not yet started — highest-leverage gap since it appears in every paper.

## Phase plan

### Phase 1 — Coverage (Jul – mid-Aug, 6 weeks)
One unit per subject per week, each unit = Topic Deep-Dive + 3-level sheet set (L1/L2/L3) + one condensed olympiad-format paper (SOF MCQ style).
- **English first-heavy** (IEO lands 3–4 weeks before the others): verbs & tenses → articles & prepositions → adjectives → vocabulary → comprehension drills.
- **Science**: food → human body (full) → matter & materials → water & air → transport & communication → earth & universe.
- **Math**: division (after tables Stage 4) → fractions → measurement → time & money → geometry + data handling. Mental math tracks (addition + multiplication) continue as the daily 15-min engine — they ARE the IMO speed prep.
- **Logical Reasoning**: one 20-min drill sheet per week, rotating question types. Start week 1.

### Phase 2 — Mixed practice (mid-Aug – mid-Sep, 4 weeks)
Full-syllabus mixed papers per subject weekly (condensed 18-question format), mistake journal drives one targeted revision sheet per subject per week. Weak NSO/IMO units get a second pass.

### Phase 3 — Mock season (from mid-Sep, staggered by exam)
Full-length 35+5, 60-minute timed mocks in real format, one per week per subject in the 3 weeks before that subject's date:
- IEO mocks: weeks of Sep 7, 14, 21 → exam 30 Sep
- IMO mocks: weeks of Sep 28, Oct 5, 12 → exam 23 Oct
- NSO mocks: weeks of Oct 5, 12, 19 → exam 30 Oct
Score each in `_olympiad-progress.md`; last week before each exam = confidence building only (L1-level + best topics), no new material.

### Phase 4 — Post-exam / Level 2 (Nov – Feb)
If qualified for Level 2 (Feb 2027): 4-week sprint per qualifying subject, Achiever's-section weighted. Otherwise: mental math tracks continue; bank material for Class IV.
