# IEO Class III — SOF Pattern Reference
# Source: Compiled from SOF IEO sample papers and official pattern
# Last updated: 2026-04-25 (run "update SOF papers" to refresh from sofworld.org)

## Paper Structure (full SOF IEO paper)
- Section 1 (Word and Structure Knowledge): 15 questions × 1 mark
- Section 2 (Reading): 5 questions × 1 mark (based on a passage)
- Section 3 (Spoken and Written Expression): 10 questions × 1 mark
- Section 4 (Achiever's Section): 5 questions × 3 marks
- Total: 35 questions, 50 marks, 60 minutes

## Our practice paper condensed format:
- Section A (Logical Reasoning): 4 MCQs × 1 mark
- Section B (English Content): 12 MCQs × 1 mark
- Section C (Achiever's): 2 MCQs × 2 marks

## Section A — Logical Reasoning
Same pattern as other SOF papers. English-flavoured:
- Word analogy: "Happy : Sad :: Hot : ___"  (a) Warm  (b) Cold  (c) Cool  (d) Heat
- Alphabet series: "Z, X, V, T, ___"  (a) R  (b) S  (c) Q  (d) U
- Odd one out: "Cat, Dog, Rose, Fish — which doesn't belong?"

## Section B — English Content (topic-specific examples)

### Nouns
- "Which word is a PROPER noun?"  (a) city  (b) river  (c) Ganga  (d) mountain
- "A group of lions is called a ___"  (a) flock  (b) pride  (c) herd  (d) pack
- "The ___ of students were making noise. (collective noun)"  (a) group  (b) class  (c) bunch  (d) crowd
- "Which sentence has a noun used as a subject?"  (a) Run fast  (b) The dog barked  (c) Slowly and quietly  (d) Very big

### Pronouns  
- "She ___ her homework every day."  (a) do  (b) does  (c) done  (d) doing
- Replace the noun: "Rahul went to the market. ___ bought vegetables."  (a) She  (b) They  (c) He  (d) It

### Adjectives
- "The ___ elephant walked slowly."  (a) quickly  (b) huge  (c) running  (d) happily
- "Which word is an adjective: beautiful, run, slowly, jump?"

### Verbs & Tenses
- "Yesterday, she ___ to school."  (a) goes  (b) go  (c) went  (d) going
- "They ___ playing cricket right now."  (a) is  (b) are  (c) was  (d) were

### Articles
- "___ umbrella was left on the bus."  (a) A  (b) An  (c) The  (d) No article

### Prepositions
- "The cat is hiding ___ the table."  (a) on  (b) under  (c) above  (d) beside

### Vocabulary
- "Which word means the same as 'happy'?"  (a) Sad  (b) Angry  (c) Joyful  (d) Tired
- "The opposite of 'noisy' is ___"  (a) loud  (b) quiet  (c) busy  (d) messy
- Spelling: "Which is spelled correctly: (a) becuase  (b) because  (c) becaus  (d) beacuse"

### Reading Comprehension (short passage — 4 lines + 2 questions)
Example passage: "Mia loved to paint. Every Sunday, she would sit by the window and paint the garden. Her favourite colours were green and yellow. Her paintings made everyone smile."
- "What did Mia love to do?"  (a) Sing  (b) Dance  (c) Paint  (d) Cook
- "When did Mia paint?"  (a) Every day  (b) Every Sunday  (c) Every Saturday  (d) In the evenings

## Achiever's Section (harder)
- Double-blank: "She is ___ honest girl. She always speaks ___ truth."  (a) a, a  (b) an, the  (c) a, the  (d) an, a
- Error spotting: "Find the error: 'The childrens played in the park.'"  (a) The  (b) childrens  (c) played  (d) in
- Passage inference: "Based on the passage, what can we understand about Mia?" — requires reading between the lines
- Rearrange: "Rearrange to make a sentence: market / went / the / to / she"

## Style notes (from real IEO papers)
- Grammar questions test functional knowledge, not memorised rules — always in sentence context
- Vocabulary is at CBSE Class III level: common antonyms, synonyms, one-word substitutes
- Comprehension passages are 4–6 lines, simple vocabulary, familiar settings (home, school, nature)
- Options for grammar questions always include the most common mistake as a distractor
- Achiever's questions test two grammar concepts at once (e.g., article + preposition in one sentence)
- Punctuation and spelling tested through error-spotting, not direct questions
