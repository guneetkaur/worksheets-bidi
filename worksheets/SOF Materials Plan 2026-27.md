Companion to `SOF Prep Plan 2026-27.md`. Every document, its folder, and its generation week. **~114 documents total** across 20 weeks, all in the locked house style, PDF.

> **STATUS (2026-07-05): ALL items below generated, ahead of schedule.** The 95 worksheets/papers (reasoning drills, all unit L1/L2/L3 + mini papers, mixed papers, mocks) are interactive MCQ sheets inside `Ambar Worksheets App.html` (project root) — each also printable via its Print buttons. ## What a "unit pack" contains (5 documents)

1. **Topic Deep-Dive** — 2–3 page parent-readable notes (like the existing SOF Unit 1 deep-dive)
2. **Practice Sheet L1 (Textbook)** — direct recall
3. **Practice Sheet L2 (Mixed)** — 70/20/10 recall/application/HOTS
4. **Practice Sheet L3 (Stretch)** — application-heavy, Achiever's-style
5. **Mini Olympiad Paper** — condensed SOF format: 4 reasoning + 12 content + 2 Achiever's MCQs, timed 25 min
