# SOF GKO Unit 1 — Weekly Prep Schedule
**For Ambar, Class III | Recommended Duration: 3–4 weeks**

---

## OVERVIEW

**Estimated SOF Exam Date:** August 2026 (adjust based on actual date)

**Prep Strategy:**
- Week 1: Learn topics + L1 practice (textbook level)
- Week 2: Mixed difficulty + weak areas + L2 practice
- Week 3: Stretch difficulty + full exam simulations
- Week 4: Final revision (optional, for confidence)

**Time per week:** 3–4 hours (30–45 min daily, 5 days)

**Why shorter than Science?** GK is fact-based recall; less conceptual depth, fewer topics to master.

## DAILY PRACTICE TEMPLATE

**Each day, allocate time as follows:**

```
WARMUP (5 min)
  ↓
LEARN/REVIEW (20–25 min)
  ↓
PRACTICE (15–20 min)
  ↓
REVIEW & DISCUSS (5–10 min)
```

### Sample Day Structure

**Tuesday (Example: Capitals topic)**
- **Warmup (5 min):** Recall: "Name 5 Indian capitals"
- **Learn (20 min):** Read Geography section, focus on capitals
- **Practice (15 min):** Answer L1 worksheet Q1–3 (geography questions)
- **Review (5 min):** Discuss answers, correct mistakes

---

## WEAK AREA TARGETING SYSTEM

### When Ambar Gets a Question Wrong:

1. **Record the mistake:** Which question? Which topic? Why did they get it wrong?
   - Example: "Q6 - Forgot that Bangalore is the capital of Karnataka (confused with Hyderabad)"

2. **Identify the pattern:** Is it the same topic repeatedly?
   - Example: "Always confuses south Indian capitals"

3. **Re-teach + Re-practice:**
   - Use Deep-Dive to re-read that section
   - Do 3–5 similar practice questions on that topic
   - Quiz Ambar verbally next day

4. **Track improvement:**
   - Same question type in next week should show improvement

## EXAM DAY CHECKLIST (30 min before exam)

- [ ] Ambar has had good sleep last night
- [ ] Breakfast eaten (not too heavy)
- [ ] Pencils, eraser, sharpener in bag
- [ ] Admit card ready
- [ ] Reach exam center 15 min early
- [ ] Use washroom before exam

### During Exam (30 minutes)
- **0–2 min:** Read all questions (quick scan)
- **2–6 min:** Solve Section A (Logical Reasoning) — 4 Qs
- **6–24 min:** Solve Section B (GK) — 12 Qs
- **24–28 min:** Solve Section C (Achiever's) — 2 Qs
- **28–30 min:** Review all answers (if time permits)

**Strategy:** Don't get stuck on one question. If unsure, mark and move ahead. Come back if time remains.

### Tips for Success
1. **Flashcards:** Make or download cards for capitals, symbols, dates
2. **Mnemonics:** Create memory tricks (e.g., "DreamS" = Delhi, Raipur, Eagle...hmm, this is hard 😊)
3. **Posters:** Draw India map with capitals, festivals calendar
4. **Chanting:** Repeat facts aloud (auditory learning)
5. **Teaching:** When Ambar can explain a fact to you, it's clear!
6. **Current events:** Link festivals/history to real-world examples

### Parent/Tutor Role
- Track progress weekly
- Make studying interactive (quiz, drawing, discussion)
- Celebrate memorization wins
- Keep exam-day stress low
- Focus on learning, not just scores
