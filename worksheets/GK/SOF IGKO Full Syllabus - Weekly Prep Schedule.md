## WEAK AREA TARGETING SYSTEM

When Ambar gets a question wrong:
1. **Log it:** Which question? Which topic? Why was it wrong?
2. **Identify pattern:** Same topic repeated? Same question type?
3. **Re-teach:** Use Deep-Dive to re-read section, do 3–5 similar questions
4. **Quiz next day:** Verbal quiz on that topic to reinforce
5. **Track progress:** Next L2/L3 should show improvement

## STUDY TIPS

**Make learning fun:**
- Draw maps, charts, timelines
- Play flashcard games (speed recall)
- Act out scenarios (family roles, professions)
- Tell stories in your own words
- Discuss with family (they love hearing what you learn!)

**Retention tricks:**
- Say facts aloud (hearing helps memory)
- Create silly stories to remember (rhyming capitals)
- Teach someone else (explaining cements knowledge)
- Review before bedtime (brain processes sleep)
