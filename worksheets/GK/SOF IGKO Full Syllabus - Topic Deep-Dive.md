## SECTION 1: ME AND MY SURROUNDINGS

### Family & Home
- **Family structure:** Father, mother, siblings, grandparents, cousins
- **Family roles:** Parents provide love, care, education; siblings are friends and companions
- **Home:** A safe place to live, basic needs (food, water, shelter, clothing)
- **Responsibilities:** Keeping room clean, helping parents, listening to elders

### School & Community
- **School:** Place of learning, teachers teach, friends play together, principal is head
- **School rules:** Follow discipline, respect teachers, help classmates
- **Community:** Neighborhood, people living together, postman, milkman, police
- **Professions:** Doctor, teacher, farmer, shopkeeper, engineer, police officer

### Daily Routines
- **Hygiene:** Brush teeth twice, bathe daily, wash hands before eating
- **Good habits:** Sleep on time, eat healthy food, listen to parents
- **Bad habits:** Lying, hitting, wasting food, staying up late

## SECTION 2: PLANTS AND ANIMALS (Extended)

### Plant Types & Parts
- **Types:** Trees (neem, mango, banyan), shrubs (rose, hibiscus), herbs (mint, basil), climbers (money plant), creepers (watermelon)
- **Parts:** Root (absorbs water), stem (supports plant), leaf (makes food), flower (reproduces), fruit (protects seeds)
- **Uses:** Food (rice, dal, vegetables), medicine (tulsi), wood (furniture), oxygen

### Animals & Classification
- **Habitats:** Land (lion, deer, cow), water (fish, whale), air (eagle, parrot)
- **Food habits:** Herbivore (cow, deer), carnivore (tiger, eagle), omnivore (human, bear, crow)
- **Groups:** School of fish, flock of birds, herd of cattle, colony of ants
- **Pets:** Dog, cat, rabbit, parrot, goldfish
- **Wild animals:** Tiger, lion, elephant, zebra, cheetah
- **Birds:** Sparrow, crow, peacock, pigeon, owl

### Body Parts & Adaptation
- **Fish:** Fins (swim), gills (breathe underwater), scales (protection)
- **Birds:** Wings (fly), feathers (insulation), beaks (eat)
- **Mammals:** Four legs (move), fur (warmth), different teeth (eat varied food)

## SECTION 4: SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY

### Basic Physics
- **Light:** Makes things visible, creates shadows, travels straight
- **Heat:** Makes things warm, comes from sun, fire, friction
- **Sound:** Made by vibrations, travels through air/water, we hear with ears
- **Electricity:** Powers lights, fans, TV; comes from power plants or batteries
- **Magnets:** Attract iron, have north and south poles

### Earth & Space
- **Earth:** Round planet, rotates (day-night), revolves around sun (seasons)
- **Moon:** Orbits earth, light comes from sun, causes tides
- **Sun:** Star, center of solar system, gives light and heat
- **Stars:** Distant suns, twinkle at night
- **Planets:** Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune

### Simple Machines
- **Lever:** Simple stick to lift heavy objects (seesaw, crowbar)
- **Pulley:** Rope over wheel (well, lifting)
- **Incline:** Slope to move things up (ramp)
- **Wheel:** Round object that rolls (bicycle, car)

### Inventions
- **Wheel:** Movement, transport
- **Fire:** Warmth, cooking, light
- **Paper:** Writing, books
- **Printing press:** Books, newspapers
- **Bulb (Edison):** Electric light
- **Telephone (Bell):** Communication
- **Steam engine:** Industry, trains

## SECTION 6: EARTH AND ITS ENVIRONMENT

### Geography Features
- **Mountains:** High land (Himalayas, Western Ghats), provide water, minerals
- **Rivers:** Water bodies (Ganges, Brahmaputra, Nile), provide water, fish, transport
- **Oceans:** Saltwater (Atlantic, Pacific, Indian), home to fish, whales
- **Deserts:** Dry land (Sahara, Thar), hot, sandy, few plants
- **Forests:** Trees and plants (tropical, temperate), home to animals
- **Islands:** Land surrounded by water (Sri Lanka, Madagascar)

### Weather & Seasons
- **Weather:** Sun (hot, light), rain (wet, water), wind (air movement), snow (cold)
- **Seasons:** Summer (hot), winter (cold), spring (new growth), autumn (leaves fall)
- **Monsoon:** Heavy rain season in India (Jun-Sep)
- **Climate:** Long-term weather pattern of a region

### Water Cycle
- **Evaporation:** Water from sea/lakes becomes vapor (heat from sun)
- **Condensation:** Vapor cools and forms clouds
- **Precipitation:** Rain/snow falls from clouds
- **Collection:** Water goes to rivers, seas (cycle repeats)

### Environment Care
- **Pollution:** Dirty air (smoke), dirty water (waste), noise
- **Conservation:** Save water, save electricity, plant trees, recycle
- **Recycling:** Reuse paper, plastic, glass instead of throwing away
- **Green practices:** Use less plastic, walk/cycle, eat local food

### Transportation
- **Land:** Cars, buses, trains, bicycles, motorcycles

- **Air:** Airplanes, helicopters, drones

### Communication
- **Face-to-face:** Talking to someone nearby (direct, fastest)
- **Telephone:** Talk to someone far away (phone call, WhatsApp call)
- **Internet:** Email, video call, social media (Skype, Zoom)
- **Postal:** Letter, postcard (postman delivers to home)
- **Media:** Newspaper (news), TV (news + entertainment), radio (audio)
- **Mobile:** Phone with apps (messages, calls, internet)

### Postal System
- **Postman:** Delivers mail to homes
- **Post office:** Place to buy stamps, send letters
- **Stamp:** Shows postage paid
- **Envelope:** Container for letters

## SECTION 9: MATHS FUN

### Number Patterns
- **Even numbers:** 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 (divisible by 2)
- **Odd numbers:** 1, 3, 5, 7, 9 (not divisible by 2)
- **Counting:** 1, 2, 3... (natural numbers)
- **Sequence:** What comes next? 2, 4, 6, ? → 8

### Basic Operations
- **Addition:** 5 + 3 = 8 (combine)
- **Subtraction:** 8 - 3 = 5 (take away)
- **Multiplication:** 3 × 4 = 12 (repeated addition)
- **Division:** 12 ÷ 3 = 4 (share equally)

### Shapes & Sizes
- **Shapes:** Circle (round), square (4 equal sides), triangle (3 sides), rectangle (4 sides)
- **Bigger/smaller:** 10 > 5 (10 is bigger than 5)
- **Measurement:** Length (long/short), weight (heavy/light), height (tall/short)

### Logic Puzzles
- **Odd one out:** Which doesn't belong? Apple, orange, car, banana → car (not fruit)
- **Analogies:** 2 is to 4 as 3 is to ? → 6 (both double)
- **Sequence:** 5, 10, 15, ? → 20 (add 5 each time)

## SECTION 10: LIFE SKILLS

### Moral Values
- **Honesty:** Tell the truth even if it's hard
- **Courage:** Be brave, face fears, stand up for right
- **Kindness:** Help others, be nice, share
- **Respect:** Honor teachers, elders, others' feelings
- **Patience:** Wait calmly, don't rush, listen to others

### Team Work
- **Cooperation:** Work together toward common goal
- **Sharing:** Divide tasks, share resources
- **Supporting:** Help friends when they struggle
- **Communication:** Talk clearly, listen to others

### Environmental Sensitivity
- **Reduce waste:** Use less paper, plastic, water
- **Reuse:** Use things again instead of throwing away
- **Recycle:** Collect recyclables (glass, paper, plastic)
- **Protect animals:** Don't hurt animals, save habitat
- **Clean surroundings:** Keep school, home, park clean

### Communication Skills
- **Listen:** Pay attention when others speak
- **Speak clearly:** Use proper words, polite tone
- **Body language:** Smile, maintain eye contact, stand tall
- **Empathy:** Understand others' feelings

### Leadership
- **Take initiative:** Start tasks without being asked
- **Make decisions:** Choose wisely, consider others
- **Be responsible:** Do assigned tasks well
- **Inspire others:** Set good example, motivate friends

### Time Management
- **Plan:** Know what needs to be done
- **Prioritize:** Do important things first
- **Schedule:** Allocate time for study, play, rest, meals
- **Punctuality:** Be on time, don't waste time
