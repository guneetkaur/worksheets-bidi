## SECTION 1: INDIAN GEOGRAPHY

### 1.1 States & Capitals
**India has 28 states and 8 Union Territories**

**Key capitals to memorize:**
- North: Delhi (National Capital), Jaipur (Rajasthan), Chandigarh (Punjab/Haryana)
- South: Bangalore (Karnataka), Chennai (Tamil Nadu), Hyderabad (Telangana), Thiruvananthapuram (Kerala)
- East: Kolkata (West Bengal), Patna (Bihar)
- West: Mumbai (Maharashtra), Gandhinagar (Gujarat)
- Central: Bhopal (Madhya Pradesh)

### 1.2 National Symbols
- **National Animal:** Tiger (strength & power)
- **National Bird:** Peacock (beauty & grace)
- **National Flower:** Lotus (purity & enlightenment)
- **National Fruit:** Mango (king of fruits)
- **National Emblem:** Lion Capital of Ashoka (4 lions, Dharma Chakra)
- **National Sport:** Hockey

### 1.3 Geography Features
- **Longest River:** Ganges (2525 km) — flows from Himalayas to Bay of Bengal, sacred in Hinduism
- **Tallest Mountain Range:** Himalayas — northern boundary, contains Mount Everest (world's tallest)
- **Largest Desert:** Thar Desert (Rajasthan, northwest)
- **Oceans/Seas:** Arabian Sea (west), Bay of Bengal (east), Indian Ocean (south)

### 1.4 Landmarks & Monuments
- **Taj Mahal (Agra)** — mausoleum, UNESCO World Heritage site, built by Shah Jahan
- **Red Fort (Delhi)** — ancient fortification, India Gate nearby
- **Statue of Unity (Vadodara, Gujarat)** — world's tallest statue
- **India Gate (Delhi)** — war memorial

## SECTION 2: INDIAN HISTORY

### 2.1 Independence & Important Dates
- **15 August 1947:** India gained independence from British rule
- **26 January 1950:** Indian Constitution came into force (Republic Day)
- **British ruled India for ~200 years (1747-1947)**

### 2.2 Freedom Fighters
- **Mahatma Gandhi** — Father of the Nation, led non-violent independence movement (Satyagraha)
- **Jawaharlal Nehru** — First Prime Minister of India, nationalist leader
- **Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel** — Iron Man of India, unified princely states
- **Dr. Bhimrao Ambedkar** — Architect of the Indian Constitution
- **Bhagat Singh, Lala Lajpat Rai, Subhas Chandra Bose** — Revolutionary freedom fighters

### 2.3 Ancient Indian Empires
- **Mauryan Empire (322-185 BCE)** — Founded by Chandragupta Maurya, Ashoka's reign, spread Buddhism
- **Mughal Empire (1526-1857 CE)** — Islamic empire, Akbar & Shah Jahan were great emperors
- **Gupta Empire (320-550 CE)** — Known as 'Golden Age of India', advances in art, science, mathematics

## SECTION 3: INDIAN CIVICS

### 3.1 Constitution & Government
- **Indian Constitution:** Framed by Dr. Ambedkar and committee, came into force 26 Jan 1950
- **India is:** A democratic republic — ruled by people through representatives
- **Fundamental Rights:** Equality, freedom, education, property, etc.
- **Fundamental Duties:** Respect Constitution, obey laws, protect sovereignty

### 3.2 Structure of Government
- **President:** Head of State (ceremonial role)
- **Prime Minister:** Head of Government (executive authority)
- **Parliament:** Lok Sabha (Lower House) + Rajya Sabha (Upper House)

### 3.3 The Indian Flag (Tricolor)
- **Saffron (top):** Courage, sacrifice, patriotism
- **White (middle):** Peace, truth, honesty
- **Green (bottom):** Fertility, growth, prosperity
- **Blue Chakra (center):** Progress, advancement (24 spokes = 24 hours)

### 3.4 National Anthem & Song
- **National Anthem:** Jana Gana Mana (by Rabindranath Tagore) — 52 seconds
- **National Song:** Vande Mataram (call to worship motherland)

## SECTION 4: CULTURE & FESTIVALS

### 4.1 Major Festivals
- **Diwali (Oct-Nov):** Festival of Lights, celebrates victory of good over evil, lights, fireworks, new clothes
- **Holi (Feb-Mar):** Festival of Colors, celebrates arrival of spring, playful color throwing
- **Eid (Islamic):** Celebrates end of Ramadan (fasting month), prayers, feasts
- **Christmas (25 Dec):** Celebrates birth of Jesus, decorations, gifts, celebrations
- **Pongal (14 Jan):** Harvest festival (Tamil Nadu), thank sun god
- **Navratri (Sep-Oct):** 9 nights celebrating Goddess Durga, Dandiya dances

### 4.2 Indian Culture & Traditions
- **Joint Family System:** Multiple generations living together, respect for elders
- **Yoga:** Ancient Indian practice for physical/mental wellness, UNESCO Intangible Heritage
- **Languages:** Hindi (official), 22 scheduled languages, English widely spoken
- **Cuisine:** Diverse regional foods (North: naan, dal; South: dosa, idli)

### 4.3 Famous Indian Scientists & Scholars
- **Aryabhata:** Mathematician, invented zero, calculated pi value
- **CV Raman:** Physicist, Nobel Prize winner (Raman Effect)
- **Ramakrishnan Pillai:** Mathematician
- **Rabindranath Tagore:** Writer, philosopher, Nobel Prize in Literature (first Asian)

## SECTION 5: SPORTS & INVENTIONS

### 5.1 Sports in India
- **National Sport:** Hockey
- **Popular Sports:** Cricket (most followed), badminton, kabaddi, wrestling, football
- **Famous Athletes:** Virat Kohli (cricket), PV Sindhu (badminton), Neeraj Chopra (javelin)
- **International Events:** World Cup (cricket), Olympics, Asian Games, Commonwealth Games

### 5.2 Indian Inventions & Contributions
- **Zero:** Invented by Aryabhata, revolutionized mathematics and science
- **Chess:** Originated in India as Chaturanga
- **Yoga:** Ancient practice for holistic wellness
- **Decimal System:** Indian mathematicians developed this
- **IIT & ISRO:** Institutes for advanced technology and space exploration

### 5.3 Modern India
- **ISRO:** Indian Space Research Organization, sent missions to Moon and Mars
- **IT Industry:** India is global IT hub, software exports, Bangalore 'Silicon Valley of India'
- **Renewable Energy:** India investing in solar & wind power
