## GK-specific question types

- **One-word / MCQ** dominates GK. Most questions are fact recall.
- **Match** — national symbols ↔ country, inventor ↔ invention, animal ↔ young
- **Fill in the blanks** — "The national bird of India is ____."
- **Identify from picture** — flag, monument, sport equipment (use images)
- **True / False** — quick-fire facts
- **Match the flag** (if we include flag images)

## Categories commonly tested in Class III GK

- National symbols (bird, animal, flower, emblem, anthem, etc.)
- Famous personalities (Mahatma Gandhi, Dr. Kalam, scientists)
- Sports (football, cricket, Olympic events, gold medals)
- Animals (young ones, homes, sounds, groups — e.g. pride of lions)
- Monuments (Taj Mahal, Red Fort, Gateway of India)
- World capitals (India's neighbours, G20, etc. at Class III scope)
- Inventions and inventors
- Space (planets, astronauts)

## Content sourcing

GK doesn't usually have a consistent textbook. Primary sources:
- Any GK book given by school (ask the user to share photos)
- Published current affairs for kids (user shares)
- Safe evergreen facts (national symbols, planets, etc.) — these are OK to use

Avoid:
- Current events that might have changed (world leaders, record holders)
- Anything politically sensitive
