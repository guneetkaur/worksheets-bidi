## English-specific question types that work well

- **Fill in the blanks** with correct tense / article / preposition
- **Identify the noun / verb / adjective / pronoun** in a sentence
- **Change the form** (singular→plural, past→present, masculine→feminine)
- **Match** — word ↔ meaning, word ↔ opposite (antonym), word ↔ synonym
- **Arrange** the words in the correct order to form a sentence
- **Punctuation** — add commas, full stops, capital letters
- **Rewrite** the sentence correctly
- **Reading comprehension** — short passage + 3–5 questions (mix MCQ + fill + short answer)
- **Picture composition** — look at image, write 3–4 sentences
- **Rhyming words**
- **Spelling correction**

## Default question mix (practice sheet)

| Q | Type | Marks |
|---|---|---|
